# Health & Safety Statement

Sporting Chance Project Ltd recognises its responsibilities for the health, safety and welfare of staff and others affected by its activities, including children and young people, visitors and contractors.

This public statement summarises how we identify hazards, assess risks and control them so far as is reasonably practicable, in line with the Health and Safety at Work etc. Act 1974 and related regulations. It covers:

- Leadership, risk assessment and competent advice
- Safe premises, equipment, fire, first-aid and emergency arrangements
- Training, supervision, accident reporting and cooperation with other employers

The statement is available to view below. The full Health & Safety Policy is available on request — please [contact us](/contact/) or email [info@sportingchanceproject.co.uk](mailto:info@sportingchanceproject.co.uk).
