# Safeguarding Statement

Sporting Chance Project Ltd is committed to safeguarding and promoting the welfare of children and young people, and to maintaining a safe, supportive, inclusive and anti-discriminatory environment.

This public statement summarises our approach in line with Keeping Children Safe in Education 2026 and Working Together to Safeguard Children 2026. It covers:

- Our commitment to early identification and prompt response to concerns
- How we work with commissioning schools, local authorities and statutory agencies across Bristol, South Gloucestershire, BANES and North Somerset
- How to raise a concern, including Designated Safeguarding Lead contacts and local authority referral routes

The statement is available to view below. The full Child Protection & Safeguarding Policy is available on request — please [contact us](/contact/) or email [info@sportingchanceproject.co.uk](mailto:info@sportingchanceproject.co.uk).
