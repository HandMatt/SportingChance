# Equality & Diversity Statement

Sporting Chance Project Ltd is committed to promoting equality, celebrating diversity, and creating an inclusive, anti-discriminatory environment where everyone is treated with dignity, respect and fairness.

This public statement summarises our commitment under the Equality Act 2010 as an alternative learning provider. It covers:

- Equal access to opportunities, support, education and mentoring
- Challenging discrimination, harassment and prejudice, including racism and faith-based prejudice
- Support for children and young people with SEND, EHCPs, SEMH needs and additional vulnerabilities
- How to report concerns about discrimination or unfair treatment

The statement is available to view below. The full Equality & Diversity Policy is available on request — please [contact us](/contact/) or email [info@sportingchanceproject.co.uk](mailto:info@sportingchanceproject.co.uk).
