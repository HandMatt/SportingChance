We are an alternative learning provider based in Brislington, Bristol. We support young people aged 5 to 19 who struggle in mainstream school, working with families, schools, and local authorities across Bristol and the surrounding area — including South Gloucestershire, North Somerset, and Bath and North East Somerset.

This visit was not a graded inspection. We do not hold an Ofsted grade or inspection report. Ofsted looked at whether the project should be registered as a school, and confirmed that it should not.

Schools and local authorities commissioning alternative learning provision can [contact us](/contact/) to talk about a placement. You can also read about our [alternative learning provision](/alternative-learning-provision/).
